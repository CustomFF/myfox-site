MyFox is a toolkit for installing and maintaining the official Firefox
from Mozilla with MyFox tweaks. Firefox goes into your home directory,
no root needed, the system Firefox is left alone.

Install MyFox:
  curl -sL myfox.in | sh                  interactive, text form
  curl -sL myfox.in | sh -s -- --gui      interactive, graphical form
  curl -sL myfox.in | sh -s -- -y         non-interactive, default settings

Site:  https://myfox.in
Docs:  https://github.com/CustomFF/myfox/wiki
